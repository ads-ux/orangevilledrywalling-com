Drywalling in Mono

Drywall across Mono, from Mono Centre and the hamlets to the rural properties east of Orangeville.

Mono is mostly larger rural homes, many on wells and septic systems, and the drywall work runs to finishing big basements and walk-outs, additions, and ceiling repairs after a hard winter. Permits for Mono are issued by Dufferin County Building Services, usually after the Town of Mono's municipal approval. A basement apartment on a septic system has its own County checklist, so that is settled before the drywall is priced as part of a unit. Walk-out basements with large windows are where a Level 5 finish earns its cost, because the low winter sun runs straight along the walls.

Frequently asked questions

Is a rural house in Mono or Amaranth priced differently?

Not because of the address. The quote is driven by the job: ceiling height, bulkheads, finish level and access. A rural house can add travel and, for a basement apartment, a septic review the County needs before it issues a permit.

Who issues the permit outside the Town of Orangeville?

Dufferin County Building Services issues permits for Amaranth, East Garafraxa, Grand Valley, Melancthon, Mono, Mulmur and Shelburne. Erin is in Wellington County and Caledon Village is in the Town of Caledon, and both run their own building departments.

Can you finish a basement on a septic system?

Finishing a basement for the family's own use is ordinary work. Adding an apartment on a septic system is a different question, and Dufferin County has a separate checklist for it. That is settled with the County before the drywall is quoted as part of a unit.
