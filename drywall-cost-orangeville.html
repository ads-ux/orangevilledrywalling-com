Drywall Costs in Orangeville (2026)

Planning ranges for drywall work in Orangeville and Dufferin County in 2026, and a plain account of what moves the price.

Drywall is priced by the surface, not the floor: walls and ceilings together, in square feet of board. In a basement, the bulkheads, drops and ceiling height move the price more than anything else. In a repair, it is the number of visits. The ranges below are for planning. The real figure follows a site visit and comes in writing.

Planning ranges

  • Hang, tape and finish to Level 4 in a basement, addition or new room: roughly $2.50–$4.50 per square foot of board surface, with bulkheads and drops pushing toward the top.
  • Level 5 skim added to new work: typically $1.00–$2.00 per square foot where the room needs it.
  • Finishing only (board hung by others): roughly $1.25–$2.50 per square foot.
  • Fire-rated ceiling for a basement apartment: commonly $1.00–$2.50 per square foot more than an ordinary ceiling in the same basement, more where ducts and bulkheads need boxing.
  • Popcorn ceiling removal, scrape and skim to Level 5, primed: roughly $3–$6 per square foot after a clean test; overlay with new board commonly $4–$7 per square foot.
  • Asbestos lab test of a texture or compound sample: typically under $100 per sample. Abatement, if needed, is a licensed contractor's quote and not included.
  • Water-damaged ceiling or wall section, cut out, dried, re-boarded and finished: typically $400–$1,500, after the leak is fixed.
  • Small repairs (a hole, a few nail pops, a short crack): most fall in the $150–$450 range as a two-visit job; grouped repairs cost less each.

These are 2026 planning figures for Orangeville and the surrounding townships. They assume ordinary access and a clear room, and they are not a quote.

What moves the price, in rough order

  • Bulkheads, drops and ceiling height. Boxing ducts and beams is slow work, and a basement apartment also has to keep 1.95 metres of headroom over the required area.
  • Finish level. Level 5 is a full extra coat over every surface.
  • Fire-rated details. Extra layers or rated board and boxed penetrations for a basement apartment.
  • Access and protection. Getting sheets into a basement, protecting finished floors, and working around a lived-in house.
  • Travel. Rural jobs in Mono, Amaranth or Grand Valley may carry a travel charge, which is stated in the quote.

What is not in a drywall price

Framing, insulation, electrical, plumbing, permit fees, drawings and painting are separate. A quote that seems low often leaves one of them out, so compare what is included, not just the total.

Quick answers: what drywall costs in Orangeville

How much does it cost to drywall a 20x20 room in Orangeville?

A 20x20 room with 8-foot ceilings has about 640 sq ft of wall and 400 sq ft of ceiling, so roughly 1,040 sq ft of board. Hung, taped and finished to a paint-ready Level 4 at $2.50–$4.50 per square foot, that is roughly $2,600–$4,700. Walls only is roughly $1,600–$2,900. Bulkheads, 9-foot ceilings and a Level 5 finish push it toward the top of the range.

How much does 1,000 sq ft of drywall cost?

1,000 sq ft of board surface, hung, taped and finished, runs roughly $2,500–$4,500. Finishing only, where the board is already hung, is roughly $1,250–$2,500. If you mean a 1,000 sq ft basement floor, the walls and ceiling add up to roughly 2,700–3,300 sq ft of board, which lands around $7,000–$15,000 depending on bulkheads, drops and finish level.

What is the average cost of drywall per square foot in Ontario?

We can only speak for Orangeville and Dufferin County. Here, installed and finished drywall runs $2.50–$4.50 per square foot of board surface (walls and ceilings, not floor area), finishing only runs $1.25–$2.50, and a Level 5 skim coat adds $1.00–$2.00 per square foot where the room needs it.

How much would it cost to drywall a 2,000 sq ft house?

A common rule of thumb is about 3.5 sq ft of board for every square foot of floor, so a 2,000 sq ft house has roughly 7,000 sq ft of board. At $2.50–$4.50 per square foot that is roughly $17,500–$31,500 to hang, tape and finish, before demolition, disposal or any Level 5 work.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost to drywall a basement in Orangeville?

As a planning range, boarding and finishing an ordinary open basement to Level 4 lands around $2.50–$4.50 per square foot of board surface. A typical basement has roughly 2.7 to 3.3 square feet of board for every square foot of floor once walls and ceiling are counted. Bulkheads, drops and a fire-rated ceiling push it up. The number that means anything is the written one after a site visit.

Why does a small patch cost what it does?

Because it is two visits and a set-up each time, with protection, compound, drying, sanding and priming, for a small area. Grouping several repairs into one visit brings the cost of each down.

Is Level 5 worth the extra?

In a room with raking light, dark or glossy paint, or a long run of pot lights, yes. In an ordinary bedroom with flat paint, usually not. It is priced room by room, not as an all-or-nothing upgrade.
