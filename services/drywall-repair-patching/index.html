Drywall Repair and Patching in Orangeville

A good patch is invisible after paint. That takes more than filling the hole: it takes the right backing, the right compound, enough drying time, and feathering wide enough that the light does not catch it.

The repairs we see most in Orangeville are holes from moving furniture or a door handle, stress cracks running up from the corners of doors and windows as a house settles, nail pops in newer houses, and upstairs ceilings marked or softened by water after an ice dam at the eaves in late winter.

How each one is fixed

  • Small holes are backed or patched, filled in coats and feathered wide.
  • Larger holes are cut back to a clean square, a new piece of board is set on backing, then taped and finished.
  • Stress cracks are opened, taped with mesh or paper and finished, because filling a moving crack with compound alone brings it back.
  • Nail pops are re-fastened with screws beside the old nail and spotted.
  • Water damage is only repaired after the leak is fixed and the area is dry. Board that was soaked through is cut out, not painted over, because it has lost its strength and can grow mould.

Most small repairs are two short visits, because compound has to dry between coats. Grouping several repairs into one visit is the way to bring the cost of each one down, and we will suggest it when it applies.

Frequently asked questions

Can you match my wall texture?

Smooth walls are straightforward. Textured walls and ceilings are matched with a sample first, and an older texture is tested for asbestos before it is sanded or cut.

Will you paint the patch?

The repair is primed so it is ready for paint. If you want it painted, ask, and we will quote it.

How fast can you come?

Repairs are usually booked within a few days. Send a photo by text or with the form and you will get a price back.
