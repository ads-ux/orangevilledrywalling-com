Popcorn Ceiling Removal and Ceiling Repair in Orangeville

A smooth ceiling changes a room more than almost anything else. The first step is not scraping. It is finding out what the texture is made of.

Many Orangeville homes built from the 1960s through the 1980s still have their original sprayed ceilings. Textures applied from roughly the 1950s to the late 1980s may contain asbestos, and there is no way to tell by looking. So every job starts with a sample sent to a lab.

The two ways to get a smooth ceiling

  • Scrape and skim. After a clean test, the texture is wetted and scraped, the ceiling is repaired, skimmed to a Level 5 finish and primed. Messier, but no added thickness.
  • Overlay. New board is screwed through the old ceiling into the joists, then taped and finished. Less mess and no disturbance of the old texture, but light fixtures and crown are reset.

If the test is not clean, the texture is removed by a licensed abatement contractor under Ontario's asbestos regulation, O. Reg. 278/05, before we start, or overlaid where that is appropriate. We do not scrape untested ceilings.

Other ceiling repairs

Sagging ceilings, water stains after an ice dam, cracks along truss lines and old patches that show are all repaired and finished to blend in, with the leak fixed and the area dry first.

Frequently asked questions

How long does popcorn removal take?

Most rooms are two to four days including the skim coats and drying, after the test result is back.

Do I have to move out?

No, but the room is emptied or the furniture is wrapped, and the area is sealed off while the work is done.

Is overlay or scraping better?

Scraping keeps the ceiling height and costs less when the texture comes off cleanly. Overlay is better when the ceiling is also cracked or uneven, or when the texture should not be disturbed.
