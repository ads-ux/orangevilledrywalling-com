Popcorn Ceiling Removal in Orangeville: Process, Cost & What to Expect

Popcorn ceilings were standard in a lot of Orangeville homes built through the 1970s–90s, and they're one of the most requested updates we see — mostly because they date a room instantly and are notoriously hard to clean or repair invisibly. Here's what removal actually involves.

Why Homeowners Remove Popcorn Ceilings

Beyond the dated look, textured ceilings collect dust, are difficult to patch without an obvious mismatch, and can make a room feel lower and darker since the texture absorbs light unevenly. Removing it and switching to a smooth or lightly textured finish is one of the highest-impact, moderate-cost updates a room can get.

The Removal Process

The process starts with testing (see below), then protecting floors and furniture, lightly wetting the texture to soften it, and scraping it off by hand. Once the surface is clear, we skim-coat and sand to smooth out scrape marks and any imperfections in the original drywall, then apply a fresh coat of primer. If the ceiling had visible seams or damage after scraping, those get patched and feathered before priming.

What About Asbestos?

Textured ceilings applied before the early-to-mid 1980s can potentially contain asbestos, depending on the product used at the time. We recommend testing before disturbing texture in any home built before that period — if a sample comes back positive, removal needs to follow proper abatement procedures rather than standard scraping. This is a step we take seriously and won't skip, even if it adds time upfront.

Typical Cost

For a standard room with no asbestos concerns, popcorn ceiling removal (scrape, skim, sand, prime) typically runs $2–$4 per square foot of ceiling area. A 200 sq. ft. living room ceiling often lands between $500 and $900. If asbestos abatement is required, cost and timeline increase significantly due to the specialized handling involved.

Re-Texturing Options After Removal

Most homeowners choose a smooth, paint-ready finish, but a light knockdown or orange-peel texture is also an option if you want some texture without the dated look of popcorn. We'll walk you through samples so you can see the finish before committing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is popcorn ceiling removal messy?

It's a wet-scrape process, so there's inherent mess, but we protect floors and furniture with plastic sheeting and drop cloths, and clean up thoroughly at the end of each work day. Expect some fine dust during the sanding stage regardless of precautions.

Could my Orangeville home's popcorn ceiling contain asbestos?

Homes built before the early-to-mid 1980s may have asbestos-containing texture, depending on the product originally used. We recommend testing before any removal work begins in older homes, and we follow proper procedures if a sample comes back positive.

How much does popcorn ceiling removal cost?

For a standard room with no asbestos concerns, expect roughly $2–$4 per square foot for scraping, skim-coating, sanding, and priming — a typical 200 sq. ft. ceiling runs about $500–$900.
