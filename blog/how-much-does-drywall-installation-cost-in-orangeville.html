How Much Does Drywall Installation Cost in Orangeville? (2026 Guide)

Whether you're finishing a basement, repairing water damage, or building an addition, drywall cost is usually the first number Orangeville homeowners want to nail down. Here's what to actually expect in 2026, and what moves the price.

Average Cost Per Square Foot

For new installation — hanging, taping, and finishing — expect roughly $2.50–$4.50 per square foot of wall/ceiling area in the Orangeville area, depending on ceiling height, finish level, and how much cutting and detail work is involved around windows, doors, and corners. A typical 1,000 sq. ft. basement finish for drywall alone (not including framing or insulation) often lands between $3,500 and $6,500.

What Affects Your Price

Ceiling height above 8 feet, oddly-shaped rooms, extensive corner and soffit work, moisture-resistant or fire-rated board requirements, and access (walk-out basement vs. hauling material through a finished home) all move the number. Repair work is priced differently than new installation — small patches are often billed at a flat rate, while larger repair areas are priced closer to per-square-foot new-install rates.

Level 4 vs. Level 5 Finish

Level 4 finish — the standard for most homes — means joints are taped, mudded, and sanded smooth enough for a standard eggshell or satin paint. Level 5 finish adds a skim coat over the entire surface, which is recommended for glossy paint, accent walls under strong lighting, or any surface where imperfections would be highly visible. Level 5 typically adds 15–25% to the finishing portion of your quote.

Basement Drywall Packages

Basement jobs are our most common Orangeville request, and we typically quote them as a package: moisture-resistant board near grade level where needed, standard board elsewhere, full taping and mudding to Level 4, and cleanup included. Bundling the whole basement into one job is almost always more cost-effective than pricing rooms separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to drywall a basement in Orangeville?

A typical 1,000 sq. ft. basement drywall job — hung, taped, and finished to Level 4 — usually runs $3,500–$6,500 in the Orangeville area, depending on ceiling height, layout complexity, and whether moisture-resistant board is needed.

What's the difference between Level 4 and Level 5 drywall finish?

Level 4 is the standard smooth finish suitable for most paint sheens and is what most homes use. Level 5 adds a full skim coat for a flawless surface, recommended for glossy paint or strong lighting conditions where imperfections would otherwise show. It typically adds 15–25% to the finishing cost.

How long does a drywall installation take?

A single room typically takes 3–5 days including hanging, taping, mudding, sanding, and dry time between coats. A full basement can take 1–2 weeks depending on size and finish level.
