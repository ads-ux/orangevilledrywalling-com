Popcorn ceiling removal in Orangeville: test first, then scrape or cover

A smooth ceiling is one of the cheapest ways to make an older room feel new. In an Orangeville home built before the 1990s, the first step is not scraping. It is a lab test, because sprayed textures from that era may contain asbestos.

Last updated November 22, 2026 · Reviewed by the Orangeville Drywalling team

Why the test comes first

Sprayed and trowelled ceiling textures, joint compounds and patching plasters applied from roughly the 1950s to the late 1980s may contain asbestos. It is only a risk when the material is disturbed, which is exactly what scraping, sanding or cutting a ceiling does. There is no reliable way to tell by looking at it, so a small sample goes to a lab and nothing is disturbed until the result is back. In Ontario, work that disturbs asbestos-containing material is governed by O. Reg. 278/05, which sets out how it has to be handled.

If the test is clean: scrape and skim

The texture is lightly wetted and scraped off, the ceiling underneath is repaired where the scraping lifts paper or exposes old joints, and the whole surface gets a skim coat to a Level 5 finish, then primer. It is the messier option, but the ceiling stays at the same height and the light fixtures and crown stay where they are.

If the ceiling is also cracked or uneven: overlay

New drywall is screwed through the old ceiling into the joists, then taped and finished. There is far less dust and no disturbance of the old texture, but light fixtures, smoke alarms and any crown moulding have to be reset. Overlay is often the better choice when the old ceiling has cracks along the truss lines or old water stains, because those are buried rather than repaired.

If the test is not clean

The texture is removed by a licensed abatement contractor before any drywall work starts, or the ceiling is overlaid where that is appropriate. A drywall contractor scraping an untested or positive ceiling is not a saving.

What it costs

As a planning range in 2026, scrape and skim to Level 5 runs roughly $3–$6 per square foot after a clean test, and overlay roughly $4–$7 per square foot. A lab test is typically under $100 per sample. The full ranges are on the Orangeville drywall cost guide.

How long it takes

Most rooms take two to four days once the result is back, most of it drying time between skim coats. The room is emptied or the furniture wrapped, and the area is sealed off while the work is done.
