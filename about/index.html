About Orangeville Drywalling

A direct booking line for a vetted, insured drywall contractor working across Orangeville and Dufferin County.

Orangeville Drywalling is a booking line. It is not a drywall company and does not present itself as one. The site exists so that someone in Orangeville with a bare basement, a Mono owner with a ceiling stained after the thaw, or a Shelburne family who wants the popcorn gone gets to a vetted, insured contractor quickly, instead of filling in several lead forms and being called by a dozen people for a fortnight.

What that means in practice: the work is done by a drywall contractor who carries their own insurance and WSIB coverage, quotes in writing after seeing the job, and is accountable for it. Your details are not sold on to a list of contractors and you are not entered into an auction.

The site is straight about permits and asbestos because getting them wrong is expensive. The permit rules quoted here are the Town of Orangeville's published pages and its 2025 Guide to Additional Residential Units, the township permit route is Dufferin County's, the second-unit fire separation and ceiling height are from Ontario's guide, and asbestos handling follows Ontario's regulation, all as of 2026. Where something is the Town's, the County's or a conservation authority's to confirm, this site says so rather than guessing.

Some jobs are declined. Scraping an untested popcorn ceiling, boarding a basement before the insulation inspection, closing up water damage before the leak is fixed and the cavity is dry, and finishing a basement apartment without a permit are all better turned down than started.

How this works

When you call 226-241-9255 or send the form, your details go straight to the drywall contractor covering Orangeville, not to a list and not to a call centre. If they are on a job, the call goes to voicemail, and the message and your number reach them right away. Expect a call back the same day or the next working day. The next step is a site visit, because a drywall quote made without seeing the ceiling, the bulkheads and what is behind the old wall is a guess. The visit settles the board types, the finish level, the boxing and bulkheads, and whether a permit or an asbestos test is needed, and the written quote follows from that.

What we won't do

  • Publish reviews we didn't earn. When we have them, they'll be real ones with names.
  • Quote a firm price before someone has seen the job.
  • Send anyone into your property who we would not have in our own.
